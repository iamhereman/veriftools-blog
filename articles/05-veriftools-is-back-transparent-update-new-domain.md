## 1. The Rise of Malicious Clone Platforms During Our Downtime

Whenever a major digital service undergoes downtime, opportunistic scammers attempt to exploit the information vacuum. During the months when veriftools.net was unavailable, our security intelligence team identified over 40 fraudulent phishing domains mimicking our user interface, branding, and logos.

These predatory sites typically operate by stealing user passwords, harvesting uploaded photos, or accepting cryptocurrency payments without delivering any generated files. In this update, we provide our users with the exact technical guidelines required to verify authentic access to veriftools.live.

## 2. The 4-Point Authentication Checklist for veriftools.live

* Check the Exact URL: Ensure the URL reads exactly https://veriftools.live. Note the .live top-level domain. Any variations (e.g. veriftools-online.com, veriftools-app.net) are fraudulent.
* Verify the SSL Certificate: Click the lock icon in your browser address bar. The certificate must be issued via Cloudflare Edge with active TLS 1.3 encryption.
* Check for Intrusive Ads: The official Veriftools platform contains zero third-party pop-up advertisements, ad banners, or crypto-mining scripts.
* Inspect Responsive UI: The genuine veriftools.live runs on a Next.js static edge application with instant interactive controls and dark-mode styling.
