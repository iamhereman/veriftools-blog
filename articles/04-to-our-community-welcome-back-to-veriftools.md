> ### 👋 WELCOME HOME — THE COMPLETE TOOL SUITE IS RESTORED
> To every developer, QA tester, and digital creator who patiently waited: Veriftools is back. All tools, templates, and utilities are online and accessible at https://veriftools.live.

## 1. A Heartfelt Thank You to Our Global Community

During the months when Veriftools was inaccessible, we were humbled by the sheer volume of inquiries, support tickets, and forum discussions asking about our return. It served as a powerful reminder of how integral our tools have become for quality assurance teams testing automated KYC flows, developers debugging optical character recognition systems, and researchers evaluating document integrity.

We know that our absence caused genuine disruptions. Many of you had to test alternative tools that were buggy, plagued by malicious ads, or lacked basic privacy protections. We are committed to making it up to you by providing a platform that is faster, safer, and far more capable than anything we built in the past.

## 2. Directory of Restored Document Generators on veriftools.live

* Passports & Travel Documents: Full ICAO 9303 Doc 9303 compliant Machine Readable Travel Documents (MRTD) with 2-line and 3-line MRZ generation.
* Driver's Licenses & National ID Cards: High-definition 1D and 2D PDF417 barcode generators conforming to AAMVA DL/ID standards.
* Proof of Address & Utility Bills: Customizable telecom, electricity, gas, and water bill mock templates with accurate regional tax breakdowns.
* Bank Statements & Financial Records: Financial institution mock templates with calculated ledger running balances and transaction consistency.
* Tax Declarations & Paystubs: Compliant salary slips and revenue documentation testing models.

## 3. What to Expect Moving Forward

The relaunch at veriftools.live is only the starting point. We have established an active public roadmap where users can vote on upcoming document templates, request API endpoints, and submit suggestions directly to our engineering team.
