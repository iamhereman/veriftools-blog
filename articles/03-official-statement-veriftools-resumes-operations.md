## 1. Executive Incident Summary & Timeline

Between Q3 2025 and Q1 2026, Veriftools experienced an unprecedented operational blackout. Millions of visitors trying to access veriftools.net and its secondary domain mirrors encountered connection timeouts, DNS resolution failures, and server error codes. This public report outlines the technical root causes, remedial actions taken, and the security guarantees now established at veriftools.live.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Our investigation isolated three concurrent failure points that triggered the legacy system shutdown:

* Upstream Hosting Deprovisioning: Our former hosting provider abruptly terminated hosting agreements without standard migration notice periods.
* Registrar Lockout & DNS Hijack Risks: As legacy domain registrations expired, malicious third parties attempted DNS poisoning and spear-phishing attacks against our domain portfolio.
* Database Integrity Protection: To guarantee that user records and proprietary generator models could never be compromised, our security architects initiated an emergency cryptographic kill-switch.

## 3. Remediation and Modernization Steps Completed

To permanently safeguard Veriftools from similar vulnerabilities, we executed a four-stage restoration protocol:

* Stage 1: Cold-Storage Backup Restoration: Offline, air-gapped cryptographic backups were restored and verified against SHA-256 baseline checksums.
* Stage 2: Edge-Native Migration: Migrated all DNS and compute services to Cloudflare Enterprise with automated DDoS scrubbing and multi-region failover.
* Stage 3: Penetration Testing & Vulnerability Assessment: Conducted rigorous static code analysis and simulated attack vectors against our API endpoints.
* Stage 4: Domain Standardization: Consolidated all official operations under a single brand authority at veriftools.live.

## 4. Service Status Verification and Access Protocol

All services previously provided by Veriftools are currently fully operational. Users can verify the authentic status of our servers by checking the live system health indicator at veriftools.live.
